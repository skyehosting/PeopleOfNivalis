A mod for Nivalis Nights

Everyone in Nivalis has a story.

Walk up to anyone, press T, and talk to them in your own words. They remember your name if you gave it, what you talked about, and how they feel about you. And when you're not looking, they talk to each other.

The city talks

They chat, take calls, and talk about you.

Residents near you start conversations of their own, take phone calls, and mutter to themselves, with voices that fade with distance. Press Enter to say something to the people in front of you, and pick who's listening.

Conversations of their own

Two neighbours catch up, a table argues over dinner, someone answers a call, and they remember what was said.

Overheard, then passed on

Stand close and you might catch something. So might they, and gossip about you spreads by district.

Voices in the street

Lines are spoken in 3D from their heads, quieter behind you, fading with distance, with a tone that follows the talk.

Your phone

Get their number. They text back.

Press N to take out your phone. People answer in their own time, the way people do, and the city has its own social app.

  • Messages

    Ask for a number in a chat, then text them. Typing dots, read receipts, and replies that fit their day.

  • Nightline

    The city's social app. Residents post, react and comment, and remember it. You can post too, with your own photos.

  • News and talk around town

    What's on the holos and what people are saying: the same news people bring up when you chat.

  • Camera and Gallery

    Walk, look around and click: a photo of your view without the HUD, ready to post on Nightline. The street photos here were taken with it.

Everything else

A life for everyone you meet.

Every ordinary resident gets a lasting identity, a job, a mood, and a life that moves on between your meetings. Story characters remember your scenes with them, without spoilers.

Real people, not quest-givers

Jobs, families, worries and plans that fit what you can see of them, and change as days go by.

Friends, dates, and grudges

How they feel about you moves with every conversation. Friends wave you over; some ask you out; others won't forget what you said.

Out on the town

Ask a friend to go fishing, or for company by text. They come along at your pace, and might stay the night.

Run a place, feed the town

Staff and customers talk orders through. People who know you ask for a dish; carry it over and they'll pay.

Voices from many providers

OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Azure, Google, Deepgram or a local server, or Windows' own voices for free. Spoken in 3D, with a tone that follows the talk.

Your rules, your budget

Your own instructions for everyone, a switch for every feature, and a spending limit for the AI and voices.

Get started

Install in three steps.

First, install BepInEx (it isn't in our zip): unzip its BepInEx-Unity.IL2CPP-win-x64 build into the game folder, start the game once, let it reach the menu, then close it. Now:

  1. Download

    Get the latest zip from the Releases page.

  2. Drop it in

    Drag the zip's BepInEx folder into the game folder, where Nivalis Nights.exe is, and say yes to merging.

  3. Add your AI

    Start the game, then set your provider and key at the top of BepInEx\config\PeopleOfNivalis.cfg (a saved change applies in seconds), or in Switchboard's Setup Wizard (F9).

  • An AI to talk with: a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek and others, or a free model on your own PC (Ollama, LM Studio, KoboldCpp).
  • Optional: a voice provider; a Voyage AI key for recall by meaning.
  • Recommended: Nivalis Switchboard (free, a separate zip) for in-game settings.

Your keys stay on your PC, go only to the provider you chose, and never reach the log. Don't share PeopleOfNivalis.cfg: it holds them.

Costs: most providers charge for what you use, so set a limit (TokenLimit in the cfg, or the Setup Wizard). Voices start on, and with an OpenAI key they're paid from it (Enabled = false under [Voices] turns them off).
