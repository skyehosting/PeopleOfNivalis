A city that talks back

Every ordinary resident gets a lasting identity, a job, a mood and a life that moves on between your meetings.

💬

Talk to anyone

They stop what they're doing, turn to you, and answer in character, knowing their work, their district, the time and the weather.

🧠

They remember

Each person keeps their own memory of you and a feeling about you that moves with every conversation. Old memories fade the way real ones do.

🗣️

The city chats

Residents talk among themselves, take phone calls, gossip about you, overhear things, and talk orders through with restaurant staff.

🔊

Voices

Lines spoken aloud in 3D, fading with distance, with a tone that follows the conversation. Many providers, or Windows' own voices.

📱

A phone

Text the people whose number you have, read the news, and post on Nightline, the city's social app, where residents react and comment.

🎛️

Your rules

Your own instructions for every character, a spending limit for the AI and voices, and a switch for every feature.

Install in three steps

Close the game first. The game folder is the one with Nivalis Nights.exe in it (Steam: right-click the game › Manage › Browse local files).

Download

Get the latest zip from Releases.

Drop it in

Drag the zip's BepInEx folder into the game folder. Say yes to merging.

Add your AI

Start the game, then set your provider and key in Switchboard's Setup Wizard (F9) or in PeopleOfNivalis.cfg.

What you need

  • Nivalis Nights with BepInEx for IL2CPP games (its "bleeding edge" build).
  • An AI to talk with: a key from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, DeepSeek and others, or a model on your own PC (Ollama, LM Studio, KoboldCpp).
  • Optional: a voice provider, and a Voyage AI key so people recall memories by meaning.
  • Recommended: Nivalis Switchboard for in-game settings.
Your keys stay on your PC and go only to the provider you chose; the mod never logs them. Most providers charge for what you use, so set a usage limit in the Setup Wizard. Characters under 18 are never chatted with.
